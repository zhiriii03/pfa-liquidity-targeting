# Scope of this portfolio edition

## Included

- A personal academic case-study summary in French and English.
- General analytical equations and validation considerations.
- A newly written, simplified Python demonstration using fictional clients,
  artificial balances, generated scores and illustrative financial parameters.
- Checks of the public demo's calculations.

## Kept outside this repository

- Original or row-level bank data and internal extraction schemas.
- Client identifiers, personal records, contact lists or banking operations.
- Internal aggregate results and charts derived from the original extraction.
- The complete internship report, its LaTeX source, cover and acknowledgments.
- Screenshots of internal tools, fitted models, credentials and local work files.

## What the demonstration substantiates

The example lets a reader inspect and execute selected targeting, simulation and
financial-scenario mechanics. Its tests check those mechanics on synthetic inputs.
They do not validate the original dataset, fitted models, calibration, campaign
performance or institution-specific financial assumptions.

The synthetic program is not presented as the original internship source code.
No campaign conversion, production deployment or realized financial return is claimed.

## Future extensions

A separately documented synthetic supervised-learning example could illustrate the
classification pipeline. It would need to be labelled as a new educational experiment,
with its own data-generation process, validation protocol and results.

## Publication maintenance

Only deliberately prepared public files should be added. Ignore rules are a convenience,
not an audit: review each proposed change, including notebook outputs and metadata,
before publication. The original internship artifacts remain separate from this edition.
